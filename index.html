Biography

Hi, my name is Qiang Wu (吴强). I am currently a full-time Postdoctoral Fellow at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (started in August 2023).

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Statistics from Beijing Normal University in June 2023. Prior to that, I obtained my Master's degree in Probability and Mathematical Statistics from Beijing Normal University in 2018, and my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Anhui Normal University in 2015.

Research Interests

  • Survival Data Analysis
  • Statistical Machine Learning
  • Hypothesis Testing
